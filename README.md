# English Many Ways - Grades 1-11

A Russian/Kazakh learning app: Word Garden for grades 1-4 and study mode for grades 5-11.

**Open:** https://niphridium.github.io/english/

Choose the learner's grade in their profile. The unified release includes vocabulary, spaced review, grammar practice, reading, writing drafts and original exam-format exercises. The default look is «Графит» — graphite glass with a yellow accent: translucent panels with a light edge over soft colour glows, a glossy main button, glass switches, IBM Plex fonts and mono numbers; light, dark and system themes stay selectable in Parents → Settings. Short interaction sounds (tap, right, almost, sprout, next, garden grew) are synthesised in the browser, stay quieter than the words and never play over them; silent before the first tap and in the quiet evening; on/off in the header and in Settings, with a volume.

- Grade 1: 150 words, a word garden, four games, family checks and 516 ready audio files covering words, examples, phrases, chants and 44 phonemes.
- Grades 2-4: more word packs, letters, reading and spelling practice.
- Grades 5-11: vocabulary, grammar, texts, writing/speaking prompts and original exam-format practice (NIS, A2 Key, B1 Preliminary, ЕНТ, B2 First). Optional memes and slang of the day (grades 5-11 only; vetted, own stickers, never in exams or checks), switchable in Parents → Settings.
- Daily lessons play as a short vertical clip feed («Погнали!»): one card per clip, a mini-boss of already-seen words, a chest with a sticker that is shown before opening, and a clear end («На сегодня всё»). The next clip opens only on the learner's swipe; there are no streaks, timers for young learners or random rewards. Spaced review is unchanged.
- Every grade band has its own world (garden, letter town, island map, space, neon, feed look, «ЕНТ Pro») and mascot; parents can switch worlds off.
- «Тренды»: short themed extra-practice packs by age, each with an expiry date; meme and slang packs only from grade 5. Packs keep their own progress and never change the review schedule.
- Progress, writing drafts and unfinished exam sessions are stored on this device. Progress can be exported/imported as a file; there is no account synchronisation.

The first grade uses ready synthetic British audio (Kokoro bf_emma); its MP3 files need to be downloaded. Other grades use an English voice provided by the device. Audio origins and hashes: `audio/g1/README.txt` and `audio/g1/manifest.json`.

Teaching texts were prepared with AI and still need English-teacher and native Kazakh proofreading. This is supplementary learning material: passing software tests does not establish a learner's CEFR level, full curriculum coverage or an exam score. Exam exercises are original practice, not official papers. Phoneme pronunciation still needs teacher review.

Images: Fluent Emoji (MIT, Microsoft) and original drawings. Fonts: Rubik, Nunito, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (OFL). Full acknowledgements are inside the app.

This repository contains the static website build for GitHub Pages. Previous hashed assets are retained so already-open tabs can continue working.
